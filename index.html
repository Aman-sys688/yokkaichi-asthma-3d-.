HISTORICAL IMPACT SUMMARY: Cause: Unregulated SO₂ emissions from petroleum refining complexes (Kombinat). • Consequence: Widespread COPD, severe asthma attacks, and legal precedents establishing joint corporate liability.
Zero-Buffer Urban Siting

Heavy oil refineries were built <400m from Isozu fishing hamlets. High-sulfur crude was burned 24/7 without scrubbers.

Joint Tort Liability (Art. 719)

Tsu District Court held 6 industrial conglomerates jointly and severally liable, preventing polluters from evading responsibility.

FGD Scrubbers & Soryo Kisei

Japan pioneered Flue Gas Desulfurization (FGD) and regional aggregate carrying capacity quotas (Soryo Kisei), restoring blue skies.
